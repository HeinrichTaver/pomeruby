# Pomeruby - Yet Another Pomodoro® inspired utility

What makes `pomeruby` different from all the rest?
It's made by me!
It's also a lightweight TUI application.

`pomeruby` is modelled after how I use Pomodoro® Technique to schedule not only my days, but my entire week.
Keeping a database of tasks and deadlines, it can help you schedule your day to it's maximum work-life balance (or one of the extremes, you do you).

The Pomodoro® Technique book gives some pretty interesting insights on productivity that I see get lost on the hyper-simplification of `Pomodoro® Technique = 25 min focus, 5 min rest`.
This tool is my own interpretation of the book, and an expansion on the ideas presented there.


## Tech Stack

- [Ruby](https://www.ruby-lang.org)


## Quick Start
